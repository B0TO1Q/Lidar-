# Wildlight 2.0

A wildlife photography scouting globe. One search bar for places, animals, hotspots and saved spots; tap the globe to scout; drag the bottom sheet on phones. Live: https://b0to1q.github.io/lidar-/

- Wildlife sightings heatmap (iNaturalist), by group or species, filtered by month
- Tap anywhere for a scout report: blue and golden hours, sunrise/sunset direction, moon, 7-day sky forecast at sunrise and sunset (Open-Meteo), animals seen nearby with best months and latest photos
- Live sun (follows the real sun), Daylight (day/night) and Shade (terrain shadows) toggles on the map
- Species finder: curated popular and rare/elusive animals (plus any iNaturalist species), up to 6 painted on the map in their own colors, with hotspot fly-to
- Saved spots (stored in your browser)
- Satellite imagery plus LiDAR relief pole to pole: USGS 3DEP (US), ArcticDEM, AntarcticDEM, Esri World Hillshade

## New in 2.0
- Today: best sunrise and sunset shoots for the next 7 days, scored from seasonality, time-of-day activity, sky forecast and wind
- Live sightings: wildlife reported in the last 3 days, tap a dot for the photo and details
- Plan your setup: where to stand for light behind you, wind carrying scent away, and a clear line of sight over the terrain
