# Relief Globe

Interactive 3D globe (CesiumJS) covering the whole Earth including both poles: Esri World Imagery plus NASA Blue Marble at the polar caps, USGS 3DEP LiDAR relief (to 1 m) in the US, ArcticDEM (2 m) and AntarcticDEM (8 m) at the poles, Esri World Hillshade elsewhere, 3D terrain, contours and elevation readout.

Live: https://b0to1q.github.io/lidar-/
